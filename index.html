Personal-use integration

Plex Drive

A personal Google Drive connection used with rclone and Plex Media Server.

What it does

This integration lets its owner copy personal media from their computer into their Google Drive account and read that media through a locally mounted drive for playback in Plex.

It is operated by liltinylinux for personal use. It is not a public sign-up service, a media distribution service, or an official Google or Plex product.

Google Drive access

The playback connection requests read-only access to Drive files. A separate upload connection requests access to files created or explicitly authorized for this application. Authorization happens through Google; this website does not collect Google passwords or access tokens.

Only content the owner has permission to store and use should be uploaded. Media stays in the owner's Google account and local computer; it is not hosted on this website.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy for data handling and revocation instructions. Operator contact is available through the GitHub profile. Do not post private files or credentials in public GitHub discussions.
